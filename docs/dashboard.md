# Interactive Dashboard Guide

## 10 Dedicated Views & Visual Analytics

1. **🧬 Spotify DNA Overview**: Headline KPIs (908+ hours, 1,784 artists, 329 explored projects), Taste Fingerprint 8-axis radar, taste evolution timeline, top artists, and ingestion audit.
2. **⚡ Discovery Catalysts (Flagship)**: 7D expansion rankings, downstream minutes, 30D/90D retention flags, unlocked future hours, and spotlight case studies on *Panther (Aa Jao)* and *Frappe Ash*.
3. **🎤 Artist Lifecycle**: Trajectory curves (Discovery $\to$ Peak $\to$ Decline $\to$ Revival), peak month detection, longest inactivity gap, time-of-day profile, and top songs.
4. **💿 Albums & EPs**: Enforcement of the non-negotiable $\ge 3$-track rule, penetration % vs completion %, top driving songs (% listening share), and sequentiality rates.
5. **🎵 Song Lifecycles**: Raw vs Active lifespan comparison, obsession detection ($\ge 20$ plays with fast 7d velocity), evergreen favorites, and skip behavior.
6. **🔁 Listening Sequences**: Top 2-track transition probabilities, 3-song Markov sequence patterns ($A \to B \to C$), and repeating album loops.
7. **🕸️ Music Network**: Force-directed topological graph, community clustering, and betweenness centrality bridge artist identification.
8. **🕒 Genre × Time × Year**: 8 diurnal 3-hour time buckets, annual genre migration trajectories, and artist-specific diurnal preferences.
9. **🔍 Deep Dive Explorer**: Multi-level hierarchical drill-down from Artist $\to$ Project $\to$ Song $\to$ Discovery Event.
10. **🤖 Machine Learning Intelligence**: Chronological test benchmark comparison (PR-AUC 0.7302, ROC-AUC 0.9718), tree feature importance rankings, temporal leakage audit verification, and live interactive catalog expansion prediction playground.
